**Serves:** 4–6

If you like stuffing, if you like chicken, if you like things baked beneath a buttery crust, then this chicken pot pie is flexible, forgiving, and deeply comforting.

---

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken breasts or thighs  
  *(smaller pieces work best)*
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 celery stalks, thinly sliced
- 6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 medium onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon thyme leaves, coarsely chopped
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- ½ cup white wine
- 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth or stock  
  *(or 3 cups water + 1 tablespoon Better Than Bouillon)*
- ½ cup frozen peas
- ¼ cup finely chopped parsley
- ¼ cup finely chopped chives
- ~~2 tablespoons finely chopped tarragon~~ (Sophie skips this)
- 1 sheet frozen puff pastry (12–14 oz), thawed and rolled into a 12-inch circle
- 1 large egg, beaten

*Sophie Adds*
- 2 Carrots

---

## Notes Before You Start

- Puff pastry creates a lighter, crispier top crust, but pie crust also works well.
- A thicker filling before baking is ideal because it loosens slightly in the oven.
- Rotisserie chicken can be substituted for fresh chicken to make this more weeknight-friendly.

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Season the chicken generously with salt and pepper.

3. Heat olive oil in a large heavy skillet over medium heat. Sear chicken skin-side down until deeply golden brown and fat has rendered, 5–8 minutes. Flip and cook until browned and cooked through, another 8–10 minutes. Transfer chicken to a plate, leaving drippings in the pan.

4. Add onion and garlic to the skillet. Season with salt and pepper and cook until softened with slight caramelization, 3–5 minutes.

5. Add celery and thyme. Season again and cook until celery is tender, 5–8 minutes.

6. Pour in white wine and cook until mostly evaporated, about 1 minute.

7. Sprinkle flour over the vegetables and stir continuously until lightly toasted with no raw flour remaining, 3–4 minutes.

8. Slowly add broth or stock ½ cup at a time, stirring constantly and scraping up browned bits from the skillet. Bring to a simmer and remove from heat.

9. Remove bones and cartilage from the chicken. Shred or chop the meat and skin into bite-sized pieces.

10. Add chicken, peas, parsley, chives, and tarragon to the skillet. Stir well and season again with salt and pepper.

11. Lay puff pastry over the skillet, allowing some overhang around the edges.

12. Whisk the egg with 1 teaspoon water and brush over the pastry. Cut three slits into the top and sprinkle with salt and black pepper.

13. Place skillet on the parchment-lined sheet pan to catch drips.

14. Bake until the pastry is deeply golden brown and the filling is bubbling and thickened, 30–40 minutes.

15. Let cool slightly before serving.

---

## Suggested Variations

- Replace chicken with browned mushrooms for a vegetarian version
- Use fennel or carrots instead of celery
- Add more peas if desired
- Stir in crème fraîche or sour cream for a richer filling
- Serve with a lemony green salad to balance the richness

---

## Community Tips

- Use chicken thighs for better flavor and tenderness
- Simmer the filling longer if it seems too loose
- Homemade puff pastry creates extra flaky texture
- Rotisserie chicken speeds up preparation
