**Yield:** About 2 cups of cocktail sauce

Shrimp cocktail is a simple, flexible party dish — but also just as valid for a random weeknight craving. The key is perfectly poached shrimp and a bold, punchy cocktail sauce you can adjust to taste.

---

## Ingredients

### For the cocktail sauce

- 1 ½ cups ketchup (Heinz recommended)
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
- 2–3 tablespoons horseradish (fresh or prepared), plus more to taste
- 2–3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 2–3 tablespoons hot sauce or chili paste (optional, to taste)
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

---

### For the shrimp

- 2–3 pounds large raw shrimp, unpeeled
- Kosher salt
- 1 large onion, quartered
- A handful of black peppercorns
- 3 lemons (1 halved, 2 quartered)

---

## Instructions

### 1. Make the cocktail sauce

In a bowl, combine:

- ketchup  
- lemon juice  
- horseradish  
- Worcestershire sauce  
- hot sauce (if using)

Season with salt and pepper, then adjust to taste with more lemon juice, horseradish, or heat. Set aside.

---

### 2. Prepare the shrimp

- Keep shrimp unpeeled for cooking (they curl less this way).
- Optional: peel or devein after cooking if preferred.

---

### 3. Poach the shrimp

1. Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil (salty like the sea).
2. Add onion, peppercorns, and halved lemon.
3. Add shrimp in batches to avoid overcrowding.
4. Cook until shrimp are just pink and opaque, about 1½–2 minutes.
5. Immediately remove and transfer to an ice bath or tray to cool quickly.

---

### 4. Serve

- Arrange shrimp on a platter or bowl of ice.
- Squeeze fresh lemon over the top.
- Serve with cocktail sauce on the side.
- Provide a separate bowl for shells/tails.

---

## Make Ahead

- Cocktail sauce can be made up to 2 weeks in advance.
- Shrimp can be poached 1–2 days ahead and kept chilled.

---

## Notes

- You can customize the sauce freely — more heat, more horseradish, more lemon.
- Deveining after cooking keeps shrimp more naturally curled.
- The dish works equally well as a party starter or a simple snack.
