**Servings:** 4–6  
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

Packed with flavor and easy to make, this orecchiette pasta dish is a longtime favorite — the whole family gobbles it up, broccoli and all.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 pound orecchiette pasta
- 5 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 pound sweet or spicy Italian sausage, removed from casings
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 pound broccoli florets
- ½ teaspoon salt
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- ½ cup freshly grated Pecorino Romano cheese

---

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the orecchiette and cook according to package instructions until al dente.

2. Meanwhile, heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

3. Add the sausage and cook, breaking it apart with a spoon, until lightly browned, about 5–6 minutes.

4. Reduce heat to medium and add the garlic. Cook for 1 minute more.

5. Add the remaining 4 tablespoons olive oil, chicken broth, broccoli, salt, and red pepper flakes.

6. Cook, stirring frequently and scraping up browned bits from the pan, until the broccoli is tender-crisp, about 3–4 minutes.

7. Stir in the butter until melted. Simmer for a few minutes to slightly reduce and enrich the sauce.

8. Drain the pasta well and return it to the pasta pot.

9. Add the sausage and broccoli mixture along with half of the grated cheese. Toss well to combine.

10. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.

11. Transfer to a serving platter or bowls and serve with the remaining cheese.

---

## Notes

- Orecchiette means “little ears” in Italian because of its shape.
- Farfalle or fusilli can be substituted if orecchiette is unavailable.
- Pecorino Romano can be replaced with Parmigiano Reggiano.
- Bulk sausage or sausage meat without casings works best.
- This dish freezes well for up to 3 months.

---

## Storage & Reheating

- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
- Reheat gently on the stovetop with a splash of water or broth to loosen the sauce.
- Freeze for up to 3 months.

---

## Variations

- Use spicy Italian sausage for more heat.
- Add extra chili flakes for a spicier sauce.
- Substitute broccoli rabe for a more traditional Italian variation.
- Chicken or turkey sausage can be used for a lighter version.

---

## Community Tips

Reddit cooks commonly recommend:
- Reserving pasta water to help emulsify the sauce
- Using extra garlic for deeper flavor
- Finishing with additional olive oil and black pepper
- Cooking the broccoli directly in the pasta water before combining
