**Serves:** 4–6

Find the widest pasta you can for this comfort classic from Carla Lalli Music’s cookbook, *That Sounds So Good*. This method of pan-roasting mushrooms and finishing them with browned butter creates deeply savoury, glossy mushrooms perfect for pasta.

---

## Ingredients

- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 6 garlic cloves
- 1 lemon
- 125 ml (½ cup) extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 teaspoon mild chilli flakes, such as Aleppo pepper
- 1 French shallot
- 450 g (1 lb) maitake mushrooms
- Chunk of Parmigiano, for grating and serving
- 2 cups lightly packed herbs (leaves and tender stems), such as parsley, mint, and/or rocket (arugula)
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 450 g (1 lb) wide pasta noodles, such as lasagnette or pappardelle

---

## Substitutions

- Use less regular chilli flakes instead of Aleppo pepper
- Replace the shallot with ¼ onion
- Swap herbs freely: basil, chives, tarragon, dill, parsley, mint, or rocket all work
- Use shiitake, oyster, or cremini mushrooms instead of maitake
- Rigatoni or paccheri work well in place of wide noodles
- Use sherry vinegar or white wine vinegar instead of lemon juice
- Replace Parmigiano with Grana Padano or pecorino

---

## Instructions

### Step 1

Bring a large pot of water to a boil and salt it aggressively.

Finely grate the smallest garlic clove into a small bowl. Add the lemon zest, 2 tablespoons olive oil, and chilli flakes. Season with salt and pepper and set aside.

### Step 2

Thinly slice the remaining garlic cloves and the shallot.

Trim and tear the mushrooms into bite-size pieces.

Juice the lemon into a small bowl.

Grate enough Parmigiano to yield about 25 g (¼ cup).

### Step 3

Add the herbs to the boiling water and cook until softened, about 2 minutes.

Transfer herbs to cold running water until cool enough to handle. Squeeze out excess liquid, finely chop, and stir into the garlic-lemon oil mixture.

Taste and adjust seasoning. Set aside.

### Step 4

Heat a large enamelled cast-iron pot over medium-high heat for 1 minute.

Add 3 tablespoons olive oil and half the mushrooms.

Cook, tossing occasionally, until browned underneath, about 2–3 minutes. Season with salt and continue cooking until browned all over, another 4–5 minutes.

Transfer mushrooms to a plate and repeat with remaining mushrooms.

Bring the pasta water back to a boil.

### Step 5

Melt the butter in the same pot over medium heat.

Add the sliced garlic and shallot and cook until golden and fragrant, about 2 minutes.

Return mushrooms and accumulated juices to the pot and toss to combine.

Lower heat to keep warm.

### Step 6

Cook pasta until very al dente, about 2–3 minutes less than package instructions.

Transfer pasta directly into the mushroom pot along with 1 cup pasta water.

Cook over medium heat, tossing vigorously, until a glossy sauce forms.

Add grated cheese, 1 tablespoon lemon juice, and another splash of pasta water. Toss until cheese melts and sauce coats the noodles.

Stir in several spoonfuls of herb sauce.

Serve with remaining herb sauce and extra cheese.

## Notes

- If the sauce becomes sticky or clumpy, lower the heat and add more pasta water until smooth.
- A large frying pan can substitute for a cast-iron casserole pot.
- Community cooks on Reddit consistently describe this dish as “one of the best things” they’ve made from Carla Lalli Music’s cookbook.
