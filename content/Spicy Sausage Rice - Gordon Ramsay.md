**Serves:** 4  
**Inspired by:** Gordon Ramsay

This spicy sausage rice is a quick one-pan dish packed with smoky sausage, peppers, warming spices, and fluffy rice. It’s hearty, comforting, and easy enough for a weeknight dinner.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 200g spicy sausage, sliced
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 red pepper, deseeded and chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon chilli flakes
- 250g long-grain rice
- 600ml chicken stock
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Small handful fresh parsley, chopped

---

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan or sauté pan over medium heat.

2. Add the sliced sausage and cook until browned and slightly crisp around the edges.

3. Add the onion and red pepper. Cook for 5–6 minutes until softened.

4. Stir in the garlic, smoked paprika, and chilli flakes. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

5. Add the rice and stir well so the grains are coated in the oil and spices.

6. Pour in the chicken stock and season with salt and pepper.

7. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat to low.

8. Cover and simmer gently for 15–18 minutes, or until the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed.

9. Remove from the heat and let stand for a few minutes with the lid on.

10. Fluff the rice with a fork and stir through the chopped parsley before serving.

---

## Notes

- Chorizo works especially well in this recipe.
- Adjust chilli flakes depending on preferred spice level.
- A squeeze of lemon at the end brightens the flavors nicely.
- Peas or spinach can be added near the end for extra vegetables.

---

## Variations

- Use chicken sausage instead of pork sausage
- Add prawns for a seafood variation
- Stir through grated parmesan for a richer finish
- Replace parsley with coriander for a fresher flavor

---

## Storage

- Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days.
- Reheat gently with a splash of water or stock.
- Not ideal for freezing due to rice texture changes.
