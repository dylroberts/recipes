**Prep Time:** 10 mins  
**Cook Time:** 1 hr  
**Total Time:** 1 hr 10 mins  
**Servings:** 10 slices  

Chocolate Chip Banana Bread is wonderfully moist and loaded with ripe bananas and melty morsels of chocolate chips.

---

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup unsalted butter (8 Tbsp), softened
- 2/3 cup granulated sugar (1/3 White + 1/3 Brown)
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 3 medium/large bananas, very ripe (about 1 3/4 cups mashed)
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips, divided  
  *(mini chocolate chips or chunks also work)*

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350˚F. Butter and flour a standard loaf pan (9.25" x 5.25" x 2.75").

2. In a mixing bowl, cream together the softened butter and sugar. Add the lightly beaten eggs.

3. Mash the bananas with a fork until they resemble chunky applesauce. Add bananas and vanilla extract to the bowl and mix until blended.

4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt.

5. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until incorporated.

6. Fold in 3/4 cup of the chocolate chips.

7. Transfer the batter to the prepared loaf pan and spread evenly. Sprinkle the remaining 1/4 cup chocolate chips over the top.

8. Bake for 55–65 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

9. Let the banana bread rest in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool.

---

## Storage

- **Room Temperature:** Cover and store for up to 2 days
- **Refrigerator:** 3–5 days *(may dry out slightly)*
- **Freezer:** Wrap in foil and freeze up to 3 months. Thaw at room temperature for about 3 hours.

---

## Notes

- The batter should be thick with small banana lumps.
- Very ripe bananas produce the best flavor and texture.
- Mini chocolate chips can be used for more even chocolate distribution.
