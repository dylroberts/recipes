## Tortellini Soup with Italian Sausage and Kale

**Serves:** 6  
**Prep Time:** 10 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 25 minutes  
**Total Time:** 35 minutes

This hearty tortellini soup is loaded with Italian sausage, kale, tomatoes, and cheese tortellini in a rich tomato broth. Cozy, filling, and perfect for weeknights.

---

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 pound Italian sausage
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 6 cups chicken stock
- 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 parmesan rind (optional)
- 1 bunch kale, stems removed and chopped
- 20 ounces refrigerated cheese tortellini
- Freshly grated parmesan cheese, for serving

---

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat.

2. Add the sausage and cook, breaking it apart with a spoon, until browned and cooked through, about 8 minutes.

3. Add the onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

4. Stir in the garlic, salt, and red pepper flakes. Cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

5. Add the chicken stock, crushed tomatoes, and parmesan rind if using.

6. Bring the soup to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.

7. Stir in the kale and tortellini.

8. Cook until the tortellini is tender and floating, about 3–5 minutes.

9. Remove the parmesan rind.

10. Taste and adjust seasoning as needed.

11. Serve hot with freshly grated parmesan cheese.

---

## Notes

- Mild or spicy Italian sausage both work well.
- Spinach can be substituted for kale.
- Frozen tortellini may require additional cooking time.
- Parmesan rind adds extra richness and depth to the broth.

---

## Storage

- Refrigerate leftovers for up to 4 days.
- Tortellini will continue absorbing broth as it sits.
- Add extra stock when reheating if needed.
- Freezing is best before adding tortellini.

---

## Variations

- Use turkey sausage for a lighter version.
- Add white beans for extra protein and texture.
- Stir in cream for a richer tomato broth.
- Substitute spinach or Swiss chard for kale.
