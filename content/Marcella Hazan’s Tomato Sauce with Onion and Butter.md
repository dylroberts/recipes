**Serves:** 4–6  
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 45 minutes

This famously simple tomato sauce from Marcella Hazan uses just four ingredients — tomatoes, butter, onion, and salt — to create one of the richest and most beloved pasta sauces ever published.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups tomatoes, in addition to their juices  
  *(for example, a 28-ounce can of whole peeled tomatoes)*
- 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 onion, peeled and cut in half
- Salt, to taste

*Sophie Adds*
- Handful of basil 

---

## Instructions

1. Combine the tomatoes, their juices, butter, and onion halves in a saucepan over medium heat.

2. Bring to a simmer.

3. Reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer and cook uncovered for about 45 minutes.

4. Stir occasionally, crushing the tomatoes with the back of a wooden spoon as they soften.

5. Continue simmering until droplets of fat separate from the tomatoes and the sauce thickens slightly.

6. Remove and discard the onion.

7. Taste and season generously with salt.

8. Serve tossed with hot pasta and grated Parmesan cheese if desired.

---

## Notes

- San Marzano tomatoes are highly recommended for the best flavor.
- The onion is not chopped — it infuses the sauce while cooking and is removed before serving.
- Butter gives the sauce its signature richness and silky texture.
- The sauce pairs especially well with spaghetti, rigatoni, or gnocchi.

---

## Variations

- Add crushed red pepper flakes for gentle heat
- Stir in fresh basil at the end
- Add garlic for a more traditional Italian-American flavor
- Blend the sauce for a smoother texture

---

## Storage

- Refrigerate in an airtight container for up to 5 days.
- Freeze for up to 3 months.
- Reheat gently over low heat.

---

## Community Tips

Home cooks frequently recommend:
- Simmering longer for deeper flavor
- Using high-quality canned tomatoes
- Doubling the recipe for freezing
- Tossing pasta directly in the sauce with pasta water before serving

---

## Why It’s Famous

Marcella Hazan first published this recipe in *Essentials of Classic Italian Cooking*. The recipe became iconic because it demonstrates how a few high-quality ingredients can produce extraordinary flavor with minimal effort.
